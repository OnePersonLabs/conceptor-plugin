**Independent review.** The complete owner is not accepted for integration. Its generic temporal-state recovery rejects a valid state produced by its own retained projection. The tested bank uses a different seed/source replay path; that path's passing cases remain valid evidence. The review also retained a numerical qualification gap: exact Gaussian construction preserves positive semidefinite covariance, but that reasoning alone does not establish roundoff and singular-support behavior throughout the binary64 workload.
