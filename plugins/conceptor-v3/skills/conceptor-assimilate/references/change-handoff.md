# Change intent boundary

Read this when a candidate is mature enough to ground in current Conceptor meaning or when preparing a handoff. Assimilation topic notes remain outside `.conceptor/model/`; a mature candidate supplies interpretation evidence to Conceptor, not an accepted record.

Before forming a Conceptor proposal, identify the intended repository and requested outcome. Read its instructions and use the [shared operation contract](../../../references/operation-contract.md) for a bounded `context` request. Inspect candidates, direct obligations, decisions, disclosure counts, unknowns, and retrieval frontier. A similarly named assimilation topic is not an identity resolution. Retrieve current exact identities and hashes only when Conceptor's change contract calls for them; never invent them from the Markdown fileset.

Apply this route prospectively when the proposed implementation changes assimilation itself: its skill instructions, supporting workflow, or another project capability. Establish the relevant accepted meaning and any required authorized revision before implementation edits; recording the change in Conceptor afterward does not satisfy that ordering. Reuse existing authorization and accepted meaning when they already cover the change. Source capture, interpretation, and ordinary topic-note revisions remain assimilation-owned intake work, so their occurrence alone is not a reason to create a Conceptor change.

The change intent brief should be readable without intake attachments or source-ID citations. Include the desired outcome, scope, current state to verify, proposed difference, preservation obligations, observable distinguishing scenarios, dependencies and interacting concerns, alternatives, reasons, and consequential uncertainties. Label observed behavior, accepted user intent, and unverified hypotheses separately. Explain a source-coverage gap when it could alter the proposal, without requiring the reader to recover the unavailable source. Keep exact evidence locators in intake for later verification; do not let a source handoff's citation rules determine the brief's form.

If the user's endpoint is design or analysis, hand off the brief without creating canonical state or implementing it. If canonical revision is authorized, use `$conceptor` and its [strict generated proposal schema](../../../references/proposal-schema.md). Conceptor's owner resolves whether to reuse, revise, or establish a requirement, scenario, concept record, decision, or other typed identity. Conceptor's `change.capture` and `change.plan` reveal before/after meaning and planning consequences for review. Approval and application follow the user's authorization and Conceptor's own operation contract; assimilation cannot shortcut them.

Record accepted, amended, deferred, or rejected results as new evidence in assimilation when they change its working synthesis. That update is explicit and one-way at each boundary; there is no automatic dual-write or shared identity map. A proposed change to Conceptor's concept system is just another subject to assess through this process, not a reason to run assimilation on Conceptor's typed records as if they were its own topic notes.
